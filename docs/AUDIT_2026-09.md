# Game Audit — September 2026

A pass over the game as it plays, not as it is described: the gameplay and
animation systems, the character rigs, the battle system, and the layout of
the Wave and Missions boards. The point of the document is a list the owner
can pick from — every item says what a player sees, where it lives in the
code, roughly what it costs, and (where it is a defect rather than a choice)
whether it has already been fixed.

Method: the whole of `game.ts`, `main.ts`, the HUD, menus, camera and input
were read end to end; four focused reviews covered animation and rigging,
the battle system, the boards and mission generator, and the code factoring;
and the built game was driven headlessly through the title, board select,
character select, the drop, waves 1–2, a champion battle, the pause menu, a
mission on Nevarro, PvP and split-screen, with screenshots at each step.
Every finding below was verified against the code before it was written
down.

**Status.** Fixes F1–F25 are merged on `main`. Verified: `tsc` and `vite build`
clean; both headless tours (title → drop → waves → champion → pause;
Missions, PvP, 2- and 4-player split) with zero page errors; all 21 browser
suites green (`npm test`, run one suite at a time — two Chromium instances
under software rendering starve each other).

**Severity key.** 🔴 a defect the player sees · 🟠 a defect that quietly
changes the balance · 🟡 a choice worth revisiting · effort S / M / L.

## 2. Battle system

### 2a. Defects still open (recommended, not applied — each changes balance)

| # | Finding | Where | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | 🟠 **Sustained fire stunlocks every melee enemy, bosses included.** Every landed hit zeroes the wind-up (`windup = 0`), and the attack cooldown is only set when the wind-up *completes*, so the enemy re-winds next frame and is cancelled again. At the carbine's 0.24 s cycle against a 0.55 s wind-up, a Tusken, officer, enforcer or warlord in your face never swings while the trigger is held. Ranged enemies are silenced the same way: every bolt's knockback stagger sets `volleyLeft = 0`. This is why danger only ever comes from off-screen. *Proposed:* a poise budget — cancel the wind-up only on hits ≥ 20 damage or after N hits per wind-up; exempt bosses; let stagger shorten a volley instead of ending it. | `enemy.ts:868, 1247` | S |
| B2 | 🟠 **Aim assist and lock-on have no line of sight.** Pressing aim snaps the camera onto an enemy behind a crate; hip-fire bolts converge on a hidden target and plant into the crate face; the reticle turns red on something you cannot hit; a wounded crawler wins the lock over a live shooter at the same angle. *Proposed:* reject candidates that fail `physics.raycastSolids` and demote `wounded` targets in the score. | `player.ts:2372` (`aimAssistTarget`) | S |
| B3 | 🟠 **Block does nothing against melee, flame, slams or explosions.** `Player.damage` has no blocking branch; the pane exists only as a bolt collider. Against Tuskens, the officer, the enforcer and every boss, B is a slow-walk button. *Proposed:* halve frontal melee/flame damage while the pane is up, or label it "bolt shield" in the controls. | `player.ts:647, 750` | S–M |
| B4 | 🟠 **Melee presses are dropped, not buffered.** X is an edge consumed only when the swing timer is clear; a press during a swing is lost, so combos need rhythm rather than intent. Swings also cannot be dash-cancelled. *Proposed:* buffer the press ~0.2 s; allow dash out of a swing after the contact frame. | `player.ts:2085`, `input.ts:390` | S |
| B5 | 🟠 **The warlord's parry sidestep is invisible.** +7 m/s with no stagger is damped away by steering within ~0.1 s. *Proposed:* `stagger = 0.15` alongside the sidestep. | `enemy.ts:851` | S |
| B6 | 🟠 **The boss retinue opens fire the instant it lands.** Spawned 10 m from the boss, pre-alerted, right after a pulse has thrown the player. *Proposed:* the 0.15–0.4 s reaction the spotting path gives everyone else. | `game.ts:updateBoss` | S |

### 2c. Balance numbers worth a look

- `HIT_IFRAMES = 0.3` caps incoming discrete damage at ~3.3 hits/s regardless of enemy count: a 3-bolt volley at 0.16 s spacing lands two, the flametrooper's nine ticks land three. The volley table is effectively at 50–66% of its listed numbers. (F4 exempts heavy hits; the light-hit rhythm is a design choice and was left.)
- Warlord health spans 2.2× across territories for the same title: enforcer 2100, capo 1300, officer 1200, duelist 950. The desert's "easier" champion (massiff ×3 = 900 HP, with a pounce) has more health than the crevasse's (krykna ×14 = 770) and the ringworld's (ring enforcer 624, behind a shield — the hardest to actually damage).
- Rocket self-damage in PvE is 18 at point blank against 90 to enemies — essentially free. 35 would keep the point-blank rocket a choice.
- The wounded crawl (40% chance below 25% health on every humanoid) triggers on roughly a third of grunt kills and drags wave clears, since crawlers still count.
- Flee rally distance (55 m) exceeds most `notice` ranges (30–48 m), so a rallied enemy cannot see the player and idles at its new post. With F2 in, this is now the main remaining wave-stall path.

### 3c. Rig structure

Adding a clip is ~15 lines and a `play` call, and adding a humanoid is a Rigify export with `DEF-*` names — both genuinely easy. Two fragilities worth knowing: the humanoid retarget writes top-level bones' *world* rotation as local and adds the hips bob on local Y, which holds only because every armature node so far has identity rotation (a model exported with a rotated armature would fail silently — the creature path's `lift()` already handles the general case); and the creatures each own a mixer with the same three-way blend copy-pasted four times, so a `CreatureAnimator` would dedupe that and fix A5 once. Channel discipline (lower = hips/legs/spine, upper = chest/arms/head) is by convention only.

The bugs were in placement (F19–F24, fixed). What remains is layout.

## 6. Code factoring (secondary)

The codebase is in better shape than its file sizes suggest: no value-level import cycles, ten `any`s in 31k lines, real GPU teardown, scratch-vector discipline in the hot paths. The problems that will actually cost gameplay work:

| # | Finding | Effort |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | **Five uncoordinated ways to put an Enemy on the board.** `new Enemy` + `enemies.push` + `scene.add` is hand-copied in `campaign.ts` (×2), `allycrate.ts`, the egg hatch inside `enemy.ts`, and the spawner callback — beside the one correct path, `Game.addReinforcement`, which alone validates the placement, counts the wave and puffs dust. The hatch path skips wave accounting; the campaign path skips the wall-ejection guard. This is where wave-clear and spawn-in-geometry bugs will keep coming from. *Fix:* `Game.addEnemy(e, {counts, place})` as the sole entry. | S |
| C2 | **`Game.update` (474 lines) hand-builds six hit-target lists** — enemies, hit parts, players, corpses, breakables, vehicles, allies — each setting the same 12 fields, dispatched by which field is non-null. Anything new that can be shot needs a block and a branch. *Fix:* a `Hittable` interface with `hitSpheres(out)` and `onHit(...)`. | M |
| C3 | **Player and Enemy re-implement the same body rules with drifting numbers**: hazard ticks (0.4 s vs 0.5 s), knockback, fade, capsule integration with different gravities (`GRAVITY*gravity(board)` vs a hard-coded `24 *`). Every new board hazard is written twice. *Fix:* start with `tickHazards(body, board, dt)` and one `applyKnockback`. | M |
| C4 | **Radial damage exists three times with three falloffs** (`explode`, `bossShockwave`, `damageBreakablesNear`); the slam wants a fourth. *Fix:* `Game.blast(point, {radius, dmg, push, knockdown, bySlot, team})`. | S |
