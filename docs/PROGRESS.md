- 2026-08-27 — Authored Din Djarin and Paz Vizsla models integrated, the first real test of the swap contract. The supplied Rigify exports are flat skeletons with an A-pose rest and no clips, so `src/characters/authored.ts` rebuilds them into our hierarchy (preserving world rest transforms so the skin's bind matrices stay valid) and the procedural rig drives them per frame. Three things had to be right. **Retarget in world space**: our clips rest at all-identity rotations, so a bone's accumulated locals are already a world-space delta, and each authored bone takes `delta * restWorld` converted back against its own parent — which is what lets the two skeletons disagree about hierarchy (our shoulders parent the arms, theirs hang off the spine) and about rest pose. **Mirror the source pose**: our rig labels its `L` bones on +X, which for a character facing +Z is the *right* side, while Rigify's `L` really is left; mirroring through X=0 lines the sides up and puts the blaster in the model's right hand. **Light the metal**: these skins drive most of the armour to full metal, and metal with nothing to reflect renders black — which is exactly how they first looked — so the board's sky is now pre-filtered into a scene reflection probe. Verified pose by pose (idle, run, aim, flight, melee, death) against the procedural build, and bone-by-bone against measured world positions.

- 2026-08-27 — **Hero ambient**: the player was getting lost against the station, whose cold key, absent sky bounce and nebula reflection probe leave beskar reading as a silhouette. `Board.heroLight` now lifts the player character alone — 0.34 on the station, 0.10 on Tatooine. - 2026-08-28 — **Cover mechanic.** Shooters now fight from behind the boards' boxes: `findCover` in `enemy.ts` scans collision boxes near the enemy for a hide point on the far side of a box that (a) blocks the sightline in both directions — the eye-to-chest ray the player sees *and* the chest-to-target ray `updateVolley` fires along, so hidden always means holding fire — (b) sits within 0.9× blaster range of the target, (c) has real ground under it and a walkable straight path (sampled every 2 m, so nobody crosses a platform gap to reach a crate), and (d) has a peek point off the box's edge where the muzzle ray opens. The cover loop then cycles hide (0.6–1.8 s, volley cancelled — ducking holds fire) → peek (pop out with attack cooldown clamped, fire one volley) → duck back, with a 20% chance per cycle to relocate; committed shooters run the same loop at ~2.2× tempo so the director's pressure roles survive, suppression stretches hiding ~3×, being shot in the open triggers an immediate cover search, and a flanked spot (sightline reopened) is abandoned. Two bugs found by the sims along the way: hide/peek validation originally used different ray endpoints than the firing check, letting marginal cover (the 1.5 m barrels) leak volleys from "hidden" — fixed by validating with the muzzle ray itself plus the explicit hold-fire rule; and the new station edge guard (probe the ground 1.2 m ahead of any walking enemy's velocity, stop at the lip) initially gated on speed > 0.5 m/s, which let blocked enemies *creep* off the platform at 0.3 m/s as steering re-added velocity each frame — 6/8 then 4/8 walkers fell chasing a cross-platform noise, 0/8 after gating near zero. Knockbacks still throw enemies off platforms (deliberate). Verified: 3/3 station shooters use crates with volleys only while peeking (180 peek frames, 0 hide frames), desert shooters cycle behind the homestead hut, 41 engaged hostiles with cover AI cost 0.30 ms/update, browser boot clean on both boards.
