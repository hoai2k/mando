- 2026-08-27 — Fixed two player-reported bugs. (1) `Game.render` passed `domElement.width/height` (drawing-buffer pixels) to `setViewport`/`setScissor`, which scale by the renderer pixel ratio themselves — so devicePixelRatio was applied twice and the viewport overran the buffer on HiDPI displays, shoving the character up and to the right. (2) The camera-relative right vector was negated (`(cos, -sin)` instead of `forward x up = (-cos, sin)`), inverting strafe on both stick and keyboard and putting the chase camera over the left shoulder. - 2026-08-27 — Authored Din Djarin and Paz Vizsla models integrated, the first real test of the swap contract. The supplied Rigify exports are flat skeletons with an A-pose rest and no clips, so `src/characters/authored.ts` rebuilds them into our hierarchy (preserving world rest transforms so the skin's bind matrices stay valid) and the procedural rig drives them per frame. Three things had to be right. **Retarget in world space**: our clips rest at all-identity rotations, so a bone's accumulated locals are already a world-space delta, and each authored bone takes `delta * restWorld` converted back against its own parent — which is what lets the two skeletons disagree about hierarchy (our shoulders parent the arms, theirs hang off the spine) and about rest pose. **Mirror the source pose**: our rig labels its `L` bones on +X, which for a character facing +Z is the *right* side, while Rigify's `L` really is left; mirroring through X=0 lines the sides up and puts the blaster in the model's right hand. **Light the metal**: these skins drive most of the armour to full metal, and metal with nothing to reflect renders black — which is exactly how they first looked — so the board's sky is now pre-filtered into a scene reflection probe. Verified pose by pose (idle, run, aim, flight, melee, death) against the procedural build, and bone-by-bone against measured world positions.
